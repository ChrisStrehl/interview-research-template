# Writing rules for workspace files

The user reads these files in the minutes before a call and shares them on screen with the people
they are about. Write for that.

- **Lead with the read.** The first paragraph of any file is what a reader would need if they read
  nothing else.
- **Fixed headings, filled or marked.** Use the template's headings exactly. A heading with nothing
  under it gets one line saying what was looked for and not found, never deleted.
- **Length caps hold.** Each `research/<front>.md` is at most 120 lines. Each framework canvas fits
  on one screen. The point of view is about 500 words. Depth lives in `evidence/`.
- **One idea per sentence. Short sentences. No filler.** No "it is worth noting", no "in today's
  fast-paced world", no marketing language borrowed from the company's own site.
- **Their words, not yours, for anything sentiment-related.** Quote reviews and founder statements
  verbatim, short, with a link. Themes are yours; evidence is theirs.
- **Numbers in tables, not prose**, with source and date in the table.
- **Mark opinion as opinion** with [O]. Opinion is welcome in the product teardown and the point of
  view, and nowhere else.
- **English throughout**, even when sources are German. Keep German terms where they are the term
  (Handelsregister, Kapitalrücklage) and gloss them once.
- **No production notes leaking into files.** No "TODO", no "(check this)", no arrows. Open items go
  to the Unknowns section or to `state.json`.
